# Leaderboards

## Daily

Rank players by Total Error for the current challenge.

## Weekly

Rank players by average Total Error over the last 7 days.
Minimum 3 days played.

## Monthly

Rank players by average Total Error over the last 30 days.
Minimum 10 days played.

## All-Time

Rank players by Career Average Error.
Minimum 30 days played.

## Records

- Best Daily Scores Ever
- Most Perfect Timers
- Longest Streaks
- Most Days Played

Percentiles should be displayed throughout the product and become the primary measure of player standing.
