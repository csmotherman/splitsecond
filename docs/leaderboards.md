# Leaderboards

Leaderboards should create competition without forcing premature ranks, MMR, or percentiles before the game has enough data.

---

# Daily Leaderboard

Ranks players by Total Error for the current daily challenge.

Lower Total Error ranks higher.

Tie breakers:

1. Lower best individual error
2. Earlier submission time

Displayed fields:

- Position
- Username
- Total Error
- Perfect Timers
- Submitted Time

---

# Weekly Leaderboard

Ranks players by average Total Error over the last 7 days.

Minimum Requirement:

3 completed daily challenges.

Displayed fields:

- Position
- Username
- Average Total Error
- Days Played This Week
- Perfect Timers This Week

---

# Monthly Leaderboard

Ranks players by average Total Error over the last 30 days.

Minimum Requirement:

10 completed daily challenges.

Displayed fields:

- Position
- Username
- Average Total Error
- Days Played This Month
- Perfect Timers This Month

---

# All-Time Leaderboard

Ranks players by Career Average Error.

Minimum Requirement:

30 completed daily challenges.

Displayed fields:

- Position
- Username
- Career Average Error
- Days Played
- Current Streak
- Perfect Timers

---

# Records

Records should be tracked separately from standard leaderboards.

Potential records:

- Best Daily Scores Ever
- Most Perfect Timers
- Longest Streaks
- Most Days Played
- Most Top 10 Finishes
- Most Top 100 Finishes

---

# Percentiles

Percentiles should not be displayed as a primary live stat in the MVP.

Reason:

A player's percentile can change throughout the day as more users submit, which makes it unstable and confusing.

Percentiles may be added later after daily leaderboards are finalized.
